04 / LEARN & ACT

Knowledge flows
into action.

Explore simple ways to care for water at home and in your community. Mark lessons as complete as you go.

⌁01

Find the hidden leaks

A slow drip can waste water over time. Check taps, visible pipes and toilet cisterns; ask for repairs when needed.

≈02

Protect water sources

Keep rubbish, oils and chemicals away from drains, streams and ponds. Dispose of waste responsibly.

↘03

Catch rainwater wisely

Where safe and permitted, rainwater collection can support gardening and other suitable non-drinking uses.

↗04

Make water visible

Share one practical tip, discuss local concerns respectfully, and encourage others to take small steps.
